Welcome! I am Hui Jie, a Master's student in Artificial Intelligence at Xi’an Jiaotong University.

My research focuses on **global image modeling**, **frequency-domain representation learning**, and **efficient State Space Models (SSMs)** for visual tasks, including semantic segmentation, object detection, and multi-modal image fusion.

I am particularly interested in bridging spectral theory and modern deep architectures to enable controllable, interpretable, and truly global perception in vision models.

Research Interests
======
- Global image modeling
- Frequency-domain neural modeling
- Vision State Space Models (e.g., Mamba-based architectures)
- Remote sensing image semantic segmentation
- Efficient long-range dependency modeling

Selected Publications
======

**ICLR 2026**  
**GMamba: Enabling True Global Perception in State Space Models for Visual Tasks**  
We propose a frequency-domain state transition reformulation that provides a mathematical foundation for global perception with linear-logarithmic complexity.

**IEEE TGRS 2025**  
**AFSIFormer (SynFSNet): Adaptive Frequency-Spatial Interaction for Aerial Image Semantic Segmentation**  
An efficient frequency–spatial interaction attention mechanism for high-resolution remote sensing imagery.

More details can be found in the Publications section.
